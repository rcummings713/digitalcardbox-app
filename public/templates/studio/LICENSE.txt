TheDigitalCardBox Studio collection v1 — 15 November 2026

Original vector illustrations and AI-assisted painted backgrounds created for
TheDigitalCardBox. Editable personal text is separate from the artwork.

These assets support creating and personalizing cards, invitations and
save-the-dates within TheDigitalCardBox, and retaining/sharing those personalized
outputs and the selected artwork in the application's portable archives.

The vector sources, painted-art prompts, retained-file hashes and provenance
records are included in the project's Studio collection documentation. Third-party
stationery sites were consulted as design references; their artwork is not
included. Existing imported designs retain their own separate source notices.
