Animated Noto Emoji

The Google Fonts Animated Noto Emoji animations included in our picker are by Google Fonts and licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

We downloaded the reviewed selection, resized and re-encoded the animations as animated WebP, reduced the frame rate while preserving loop timing, and extracted static PNG posters. The animations are hosted with TheDigitalCardBox and can be kept with saved cards under that license, including commercial use, provided its conditions are followed.
