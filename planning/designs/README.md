44 invitations retain the actual Canva-generated artwork and layout. Canva's AI quota stopped further generation; 56 additional invitations use original editable HTML/vector-PDF artwork imported into Canva. These are authoring layouts, not copies of commercial Canva stock templates. Original Canva-generated pages were copied unchanged; blank opening pages were excluded. The unmodified generation results and one superseded draft remain in a [separate originals folder](https://www.canva.com/folder/FAHaJkJrs00).

Canva titles on two copied collections retain the requested 18/12 count. Their actual page counts are nine each; the additional nine/three collections complete those categories. The counts above and inventory use actual pages.

The folder was verified to contain ten design files and exactly 100 invitation pages. Every included page was checked for editable date, venue and RSVP text. All original source layouts were checked for text bounds and visually reviewed; representative Canva renderings were compared to the original sources. This does not claim a pixel-level review of all Canva-rendered pages.

[Machine-readable inventory](canva-invitations-100.json) maps each template to its Canva design, page number, occasion and creation method. [Design briefs](canva-invitations-100-briefs.json) retain the planning directions. Original HTML/PDF sources and generators are in [sources/](sources/).

All 100 invitation backgrounds are now retained in the application with separate native editable text, source/rights evidence and immutable hashes.
